NearMeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 5, 2026

NearMeep lets you and your friends share location, drop beacons, and send voice clips. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

What we collect

Precise location

  • Why: Shown to friends you've added so they can see you on the radar.
  • Where: Apple iCloud / CloudKit only — not our servers.
  • Control: Turn off "Appear on friends' radar" in the Me tab anytime.

Microphone

  • Why: Short voice clips and voice drops you choose to record.
  • Where: CloudKit, readable only by intended recipients.

Contacts & phone number

  • Why: Find friends already on NearMeep; invite others via SMS.
  • Where: Phone numbers are SHA-256 hashed on your device. Only hashes are sent to CloudKit — never your full contact list.

User identifier

  • Why: Friend connections and account identity via iCloud.
  • Where: CloudKit, linked to your Apple ID.

What we do NOT do

  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party tracking
  • No selling or sharing your data
  • No servers we operate — data stays in Apple iCloud

Children

NearMeep is not directed at children under 13.
